# Notes on VULCAN simulations

We use VULCAN to simulate various atmospheres.

We also vary the solar zenith angle between 45 degrees and 60 degrees. The solar zenith angle controls how many photons are propagating through the atmosphere and affects photochemistry.

We vary the chemical network.

We simulate Proxima Centauri b with VULCAN. 

VULCAN was downloaded on 14th September 2026.
